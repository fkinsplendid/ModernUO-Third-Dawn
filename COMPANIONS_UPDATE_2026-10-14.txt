COMPANIONS UPDATE - 14 October 2026
===================================

What's new for players, and how to use it. Say the commands out loud
near your companions, starting with a companion's name ("Aldric, skills")
or "all" for the whole party. The full guide is in COMPANIONS.md.


1. SEEING YOUR COMPANIONS' SKILLS
---------------------------------

  <name> skills       Opens that companion's skills window.
  (also: spells, abilities)

  all status          The party window now has a button next to each
                      name that opens their skills. The skills window
                      has a back button to the party window.

The skills window lists every skill they have trained, highest first,
as current value against cap (e.g. Magery 82.4 / 100).

For a mage it also shows:

  - Chance to cast Recall, Mark, Gate Travel and Resurrection, in
    colour: green is a reliable cast (75%+), yellow is a gamble, red is
    not yet possible.
  - Whether the mage will cast them for you, or the Magery it needs.
  - Every place the mage can open a gate to (see section 4).


2. THE MAGE NO LONGER WASTES REAGENTS ON BLESS
----------------------------------------------

The mage used to keep Bless up on everyone at all times, even walking
around town. Now it only blesses during a hunt: in the quiet moments
between fights. Once a minute passes with no fighting, it stops.

Nothing to do. You should just need fewer reagents.


3. GATE AND RESURRECTION: NEW SKILL LEVELS AND RETRIES
------------------------------------------------------

  Gate Travel    now needs Magery 80 (was 70)
  Resurrection   now needs Magery 90 (was 80)

At these levels about half of casts fizzle, and a fizzle still uses
up the reagents, just as it does for a player. So the mage tries again:

  - Gates: up to 3 attempts ("Blast, it fizzled! Again..."), then it
    gives up and says so.
  - Resurrection on you: if it fizzles, the mage tries again a couple
    of seconds later. If you decline the offer, it waits 30 seconds
    before asking again.

Note: a mage with Magery between 70 and 79 can't open gates any more
until it reaches 80. Check with "<name> skills".


3b. NO MORE WALKING THROUGH FIELDS
----------------------------------

Companions no longer walk into fire, poison or paralysis fields, the
mage's own or an enemy's. They step around them, or wait if a field
blocks the way. If one is cast on top of them, they walk out.


4. THE MAGE'S RUNEBOOK
----------------------

Every mage now carries its own runebook, given by the guild. It starts
empty. You fill it by giving the mage recall runes.

  Filling the book
    Give the mage a blank recall rune, then say:

      <name> mark                 Marks the spot where the mage stands.
      <name> mark Minoc mine      Same, and names the place.

    The mage marks the rune and files it in the book.

    If you don't give a name, the mage writes one for you from where it
    stands: the name of the area it's in (e.g. "Britain"), "Outside
    Minoc" when near a town, or "North-east of Minoc" out in the wilds.
    Words like "this", "rune" and "as" are ignored, so "mark rune" gets
    an automatic name and "mark this rune as Minoc mine" is named "Minoc
    mine". If the book already has a place with that name, the new one
    gets a number ("Minoc 2"). Marked runes you hand over without a name
    are named the same way. It needs Magery
    50+ to have any chance, plus black pearl, blood moss and mandrake
    root. In a fight, in a dungeon, inside a house or anywhere Mark
    isn't allowed, it tells you why and spends nothing. If the spell
    fizzles, the rune stays blank; just ask again.

    You can also hand over runes you've already marked yourself. They
    go straight into the mage's book.

    The book holds 16 places. After that, new runes stay loose in the
    mage's pack.

  Opening gates
      <name> gate                 Gate to the mage's usual place.
      <name> gate to minoc        Gate to a named place in the book.
      <name> gate to town         Gate to the nearest town moongate.

    Names don't have to be exact: "gate to minoc" finds "Minoc", and
    "gate to mine minoc" finds "Minoc Mine Entrance". The mage can only
    gate within the land it's in (Trammel, Felucca, Ilshenar).

    When the party is losing badly, the mage opens an escape gate to
    its usual place by itself.

  The usual place
      <name> usual                Says which place is the usual.
      <name> usual yew gate       Makes that place the usual.

    The first place filed in the book becomes the usual one. With an
    empty book, gates go to the nearest town.

  Seeing what's in the book
      <name> destinations         The mage lists every place it knows.
      (also: "where can you gate")

    The skills window lists them too.

  IF THE MAGE DIES, THE RUNEBOOK BURNS.
    The book and every place in it are gone for good. When the mage is
    raised it gets a new, empty book from the guild and you start
    again. Keep your mage alive!

  Other things to know
    - You can't take the book out of the mage's pack.
    - Runebooks can't be opened while a companion carries them, which
      is why you pick the usual place by voice.
    - The mage keeps blank runes when it shares loot.
    - The mage only uses its own book. If you give it yours, it hands
      it back with the loot.


QUICK REFERENCE
---------------

  <name> skills             Skills window (mage: cast chances, places)
  all status                Party window, with a skills button per name
  <name> mark [name]        Mark a blank rune and file it in the book
  <name> gate               Gate to the usual place
  <name> gate to <place>    Gate to a named place
  <name> gate to town       Gate to the nearest town
  <name> usual [place]      Say or change the usual place
  <name> destinations       List the places the mage can gate to
